How SABIBOOK works

SABIBOOK is a free AI homework helper for Nigerian students. Snap a photo of any homework, assignment, or exam prep question, and get an instant answer you can actually understand.

  1. Snap a photo of your question, or upload a screenshot if you already have one saved.
  2. SABIBOOK's AI reads the question and works out the subject.
  3. For maths, physics, chemistry, and other calculation questions, it shows the full step-by-step working, not just the final answer, so you understand how to get there yourself.
  4. For every other subject, you get a clear, correct answer right away.
  5. Tap Reset and snap your next question. Your recent answers are kept in the Saved Answers list below, so you can check them again later.

Frequently asked questions

Is SABIBOOK free?

Yes. You can solve questions for free, with a short ad break every few questions. Going Pro for ₦750/month removes the ads completely and lets you save your last 10 answers for 5 days, with full control over what you keep.

What subjects can SABIBOOK help with?

Any subject you're studying, including mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, English, government, economics, literature, and geography. It's built for everyday homework help, solving assignment questions, and WAEC, NECO, and JAMB exam prep revision.

Does SABIBOOK save my questions or photos?

Your recent answers are saved only on your own device, never on our servers. Free accounts keep your last 5 answers, cleared automatically every day at 1am. Pro keeps your last 10 for 5 days, and lets you delete any of them yourself.

Can I use SABIBOOK on my phone?

Yes, SABIBOOK works entirely in your phone's browser, with no app download needed.
